# 户外装备助手界面

## Surfaces
The welcome route is an editorial scrolling experience that opens on a full-viewport dawn scene. `/equipment` is a searchable public catalog, `/equipment/[id]` shows a product and its specifications, and `/chat` is an operational workspace. Public pages pass questions as drafts. Only an explicit send invokes the assistant.

## Visual system
`app/globals.css` carries the shared foundations, `app/design.css` the compatibility layer, and `app/redesign.css` owns the current composition. The world is a bright morning over mountains: stone ground `#f4f2ea`, pine ink `#14261e`, forest accent `#2c6a4c`, deep green `#143c2b` for dark bands, and trail yellow `#e0f29b` reserved for rare highlights. The hero is a drawn scene, not a photograph: `DawnCanvas` renders layered ridge silhouettes, drifting valley mist, rising motes and a sun glow on a single canvas, capped at 2x DPR and paused offscreen. Over it sits the liquid-glass prompt card — heavy backdrop blur, a bright 1.5 px rim, soft deep shadow and a black circular send button — with a typewriter cycling Chinese trip examples; an empty submit carries the displayed example into chat. The same glass language recurs in the packing board, the comparison preview, the departure input, the chat composer and the cart drawer. Dark editorial bands (journeys, packing story, departure, footer) use light text and translucent white borders; the departure section glows faintly warm at the top right.

The self-hosted Outdoor Display subset of Noto Serif SC is the display voice for Chinese editorial headings on every surface, including product detail titles, with tight negative tracking. Interface labels, data and conversation text use the Chinese sans stack. Body copy is 18 px; secondary text 16 px; the smallest functional labels are 14 px and no remark-grade microtext appears. Prices use tabular numerals. The brand mark has its own background, foreground and accent colors, independent of navigation text color. Cards use 16–30 px corners; circular controls are reserved for compact actions. Public equipment cards use open typography beneath an image rather than an outer card border. The fixed header is transparent over the hero in ink tones and frosts to a blurred bar once the hero leaves the viewport; the center navigation drops below 820 px.

## Motion
The dawn scene breathes continuously: ridge lines drift on slow sine octaves, mist bands wander fully off-screen before wrapping, motes rise and twinkle, a light shaft leans out of the sun, and the pointer nudges layers by parallax depth. The hero canvas drifts down slightly on scroll while the copy counter-drifts and fades. The first viewport arrives as choreography: headline, subline, glass card and scroll cue rise with blur on staggered delays using the exponential ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Sections reveal once through an IntersectionObserver; the journeys grid staggers per card, and the packing narrative keeps its glass equipment board pinned beside successive chapters on desktop while mobile flows linearly. Product photography scales subtly on hover. Native cross-document view transitions progressively enhance matching product imagery where supported. The conversation panel follows new content only once transcript items exist, so the welcome view always opens at its heading. All entrance animation is gated behind `.motion-ready` so content is visible without scripts, and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes spatial animation, collapsing the canvas to a single composed frame.

## Responsive behavior
Public layouts use a bounded content width with generous desktop gutters and 16–24 px mobile gutters. Equipment grids reduce from four to three, two and one columns. Detail pages change from paired columns to a vertical image and specification flow. The hero prompt card keeps its two-line height so the typewriter never shifts layout. The assistant uses a collapsible desktop history sidebar and native mobile dialogs; on mobile the cart becomes a bottom sheet and the composer respects the keyboard viewport and safe-area inset.

## Delivery boundary
Frontend changes preserve the existing routes, catalog sources, assistant prompts, server-confirmed cart writes and native dialog semantics. The redesign lives in `app/redesign.css` and the landing components so shared commerce components remain untouched where possible; the two exceptions are note-removal edits in `web-shared` (`Shell.tsx`, `Markdown.tsx`) and the zero-items follow guard in `web-shared/scroll.ts`. TypeScript checking and the Next.js production build pass locally; the impeccable detector reports no findings and the finish review's two fixes (serif detail titles, journeys stagger) are applied. Review captures live uncommitted under `.impeccable/review/`.
