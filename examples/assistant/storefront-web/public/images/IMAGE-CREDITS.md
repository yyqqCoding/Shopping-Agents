# Scene photography

Original fictional outdoor scenes generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. No actual destination or product performance is represented by the scenery. Generation prompts are recorded in `examples/assistant/data/scene-image-prompts.json`.

- `hero.webp`: mountain valley and a tent on a grassy plateau.
- `hiking.webp`: forest and granite hiking path.
- `camping.webp`: forest clearing and tent.
- `lightweight.webp`: daypack and bottle in natural light.
