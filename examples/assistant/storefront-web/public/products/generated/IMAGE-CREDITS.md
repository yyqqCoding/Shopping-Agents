# Outdoor product photography

96 original unbranded product photographs generated using the built-in ImageGen tool. Each image depicts one authored fictional catalog item. The 120 size variants share their parent's photograph; no distinct color or construction variant is substituted.

`OD-*.webp` files have a 1024 px width. `OD-*-480.webp` files are 480 px list thumbnails. Image proportions are preserved. Exact generation prompts are embedded in WebP XMP and recorded in `examples/assistant/data/generated-image-prompts.json`. File mappings and digests are in `examples/assistant/data/image-manifest.json`.

These are synthetic product illustrations in a photographic style, not evidence of manufactured products or verified performance. Archived AR product photographs retain their separate credits in the parent directory.
